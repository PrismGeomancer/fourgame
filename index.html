How FOUR works

A two-minute game with one rule: get four of your discs in a row.

Connect

Pick Phantom, Solflare, Backpack, Coinbase or Trust. Nothing is sent when you connect.

Stake 0.05 SOL

Your wallet signs a short message that locks in your seat at the table.

Play four in a row

Drop discs in turn. Horizontal, vertical or diagonal all count.

Winner takes 0.1 SOL

Both stakes make the pot. A draw returns both stakes.

Protections

  • No transaction requests. The site only asks for message signatures.
  • No agent, no launchpad, no token sale. Nothing else is for sale here.
  • Equal stakes. Both players put in the same 0.05 SOL.
  • Alternating first move so neither side keeps the edge.
  • Draws refund both stakes in full.
  • Rules by vote. Changes go through proposals, not private decisions.

Be clear about the risk

You can lose your stake

  • Every match you lose costs 0.05 SOL. Only play what you can afford to lose.
  • Matchmaking is in its first version: opponents are house-run until live player-to-player matching opens, and results are recorded in this browser. No funds move yet.
  • Skill matters. A stronger player will win more often.

Why would each side say yes?

You

Short games, a clear prize, fair odds when skill is equal, and no account to open.

Your opponent

The same stake, the same board, alternating first move, and the same shot at the whole pot.

The board

Every finished match and every closed proposal, newest first.
